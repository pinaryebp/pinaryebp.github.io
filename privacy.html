Privacy Policy

This OAuth app ("rclone") is a personal tool used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive with rclone.

Google user data accessed through this app is used only to copy and sync the owner's own files. It is not collected, stored on any third-party server, shared, or sold. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
